## Failures and resources

1. **`ADD_WIDE_GENOTYPES`** x6: a code bug, MISC-18. It is fixed and the resume was verified on 2026-10-19.
2. **`CONCAT_VEP` and `MERGE_ANNOTATIONS`**: each first attempt was killed at the `process_single` 1 h limit, the MISC-16 mitigation. Their successful retries took 1.07 h and 1.38 h at ~100% of one core. At WGS size these tasks can never finish on attempt 1, which costs ~2.2 h of wall time. F2005 ran them in 0.20 h and 0.29 h, and `annVEP` is about the same size (2.46 vs 2.83 GB). `CONCAT_BRANCHPOINT` (0.15 vs 0.035 h) and `CHECK_INPUT_ASSEMBLY` (0.08 vs 0.01 h) are also slower. The cause is **not determined**. Three changes happened between the runs:
   - `c77072b` changed both commands (concat piped into sort, BCF intermediates in merge);
   - the work dir moved to `/mnt/scratch` (2026-10-08);
   - bcftools sort temp files moved from the node's `/data_tmp` into the task dir (`e1ad046`).
3. **`TSV_TO_TABLE`**: attempt 1 (4 slots, `memory` 48 GB, `h_vmem=58G`) failed with `pandas ... C error: out of memory`. Attempt 2 (8 slots, 72 GB, `h_vmem=82G`) succeeded in 38 min. Its trace shows **peak_rss 329 GB, peak_vmem 354 GB** for a single Python process: `filter_variants.py` does `pd.read_csv` on the whole 47.5 GB `scratch.tsv` (rchar 47.5 GB). INFERENCE: SGE enforces `h_vmem` per slot. 4 x 58 = 232 G failed and 8 x 82 = 656 G passed. This answers the open per-slot/per-job question of the 2026-10-14 review, still to be confirmed with `qconf -sc`. The `memory` / `h_rss` / `mem_free` request (72 GB) understates real use by about 4.5x, so the scheduler can over-commit a node.
4. **`TAG_TRANSCRIPT_PRIORITY`**: attempt 1 succeeded, 2.5 min, peak 13.5 GB of 24 GB. F2005 needed 6 attempts. The 2026-10-14 check passes.
5. **VEP / SPiP**: 86.7 / 72.4 task-hours vs 62.4 / 53.6. VEP now includes ReMM (expected; not comparable).
